# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of delivery.

Eligible conditions:

- Product received damaged
- Wrong product delivered
- Product has manufacturing defects
- Order cancelled before shipping
- Item significantly different from description

Refund is NOT available if:

- Product is physically damaged by customer
- Missing accessories or original packaging
- Software licenses already activated
- Product returned after 30 days
- Customized products

Refund Timeline:

- Credit Card: 5-7 business days
- Debit Card: 5-7 business days
- UPI: 2-3 business days
- Net Banking: 3-5 business days
- Wallet: Within 24 hours

---

# Return Policy

Products can be returned within **30 days**.

Return Requirements:

Non-returnable Products:

- Gift Cards

- Personalized Products
- Opened Earbuds (for hygiene reasons)
